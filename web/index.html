    The call

    Five tiers

    Assigned from the workbook: without shelter or uninhabitable housing is severe; temporary accommodation, sharing a dwelling, or an emergency stay is unstable; social housing is subsidised unless costs reach half of income; a housed whānau is at risk at 30% of income, any arrears, or two people per bedroom. Otherwise the tier is stable.

    1. 1 Severely deprived

      Without shelter, or housing that is uninhabitable.

    2. 2 Unstable / transitional

      Temporary accommodation, sharing someone else’s dwelling, or an emergency stay.

    3. 3 At risk

      Housed, with high housing costs, arrears, or crowding.

    4. 4 Subsidised / assisted

      Social housing whose cost stays under half of income.

    5. 5 Stable / market secure

      Housed, with sustainable cost, no arrears, and no crowding.
